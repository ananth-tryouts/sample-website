About Fuji

Hyderabad's Most Premium
Hilltop Residences

Step into a Lap of Luxury with Our Ultra-Spacious Residential Apartments.

Imagine mornings with a stunning cityscape view and fresh breeze caressing your skin. Unwind to a relaxing stroll in your spacious private sanctuary. Fuji is conceptualized and thoughtfully built, making this a reality.

Perched at 610 meters above sea level on a sprawling 2.2-acre estate, it's Hyderabad's most premium residential project. With only 75 homes, our ultra-spacious residences range from 7,504 Sq.ft to 10,121 Sq.ft, ensuring an unrivalled sense of freedom and privacy. A life you always dreamt of, making a lifetime of memories, in a world of your own, at Fuji.
